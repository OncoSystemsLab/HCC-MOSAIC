# HCC-MOSAIC
HCC-MOSAIC (Multi-Omics Signature Atlas for Immunotherapy Characterization of HCC) is an interactive research platform designed to explore the molecular landscape of hepatocellular carcinoma (HCC) and its association with immunotherapy response. At its core, HiSTF (Hierarchical Self-supervised Token Fusion) is a biology-guided deep learning framework that integrates self-supervised transcriptomic representations with interpretable molecular programs to predict immune checkpoint blockade (ICB) response. By integrating multi-cohort transcriptomic data, somatic mutation profiles, and immunotherapy-related evidence, HCC-MOSAIC enables researchers to explore molecular signatures, investigate mutation–pathway associations, analyze uploaded RNA-seq profiles, and generate reproducible, evidence-informed predictions and biological interpretations.
